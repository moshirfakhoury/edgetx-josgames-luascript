**Introduction to Jos Games**

Welcome to Jos Games, a collection of three original arcade-style games developed in Lua for the Radiomaster TX16S MK2 running EdgeTX.

Originally created as a fun project for Josiah (My Son), the collection has grown into a trilogy of games, each with its own characters, challenges, graphics, sounds and gameplay.

**The trilogy includes:**
- Jurassic Jos – A dinosaur adventure where Jos must collect golden eggs and survive encounters with a T-Rex.
- Jos Busters – A ghost-hunting adventure where Jos must defeat ghosts and rescue Millie.
- Jos Jaws – An underwater adventure where Jos must find treasure while avoiding dangerous sea creatures.

Each game has been designed specifically for the TX16S MK2 screen (480x272px), making use of custom graphics, sound effects and Lua scripting.



**Jurassic Jos**

**About the Game**

Jos is on a dinosaur adventure! Collect golden eggs, avoid dangerous dinosaurs, take to the skies in a helicopter to fight the Pterodactyls, and finally return three golden eggs to the nest while battling the mighty T-Rex.


**How to Play**

**Level 1 – Golden Egg Collection**

**Aim**

- Collect the golden eggs dropped by flying Pterodactyls.
- Avoid the green eggs and the Raptor. Both can kill Jos!

**How to Play**

- CH3: Controls Jos's direction.
- CH2: Moves Jos forwards and backwards.
- SH switch: Makes Jos duck to avoid incoming green eggs and Raptor.

**Level 2 – Helicopter Attack**

**Aim**

- Shoot down the flying Pterodactyls from the helicopter.

**How to Play**

- CH4: Positions the aiming pointer on the left or right side of the screen.
- CH1: Moves the pointer left and right within that area.
- CH2: Moves the pointer up and down within that area.
- SH: Fires a shot

**Level 3 – Return the Golden Eggs**

**Aim**

- Return all three golden eggs to the nest.
- Defeat the T-Rex, which takes multiple hits to kill.

**How to Play**

- CH2: Moves Jos forwards and backwards.
- SH switch: Fires bullets at the T-Rex.
- Return Eggs: Jos must be close to the left side of the nest to return a golden egg without being eaten by the T-Rex

**Jos Busters**

**About the Game**

Jos becomes a ghost hunter, armed with a laser weapon and a special Nova weapon. Battle waves of ghosts across three levels, defeat the powerful Evil King Ghost and rescue Millie!

**How to Play**

**Level 1 – Ghost Hunting**

**Aim**

- Destroy 20 ghosts to complete the level.
- Avoid colliding with ghosts, as each collision costs a life.

**How to Play**

- CH2: Moves Jos around the screen.
- CH3: Controls which direction Jos faces.
- SH switch: Activates the laser.

**Level 2 – Ghost Hunting Gets Tougher**

**Aim**

- Increase your Ghost Count to 40 to complete the level.
- Avoid the ghosts and protect your remaining lives.

**How to Play**

- Same Controls as level 1

**Level 3 – Save Millie!**

**Aim**

- Defeat Evil King Ghost by hitting him 40 times.
- Rescue Millie by avoiding hitting her with the Nova weapon.
- Avoid the ghost clouds, which cause instant Game Over.

**How to Play**

- CH2: Moves Jos around the screen.
- CH3: Controls which direction Jos faces.
- SH switch: Activates the laser.
- CH1: Fires the Nova weapon when Jos is facing right.
- Special Ammo: You have 65 Nova shots available.

**Jos Jaws**

**About the Game**

Dive into an underwater adventure with Jos! Find the hidden key, collect treasure chests and avoid dangerous jellyfish and sharks. Keep an eye on your air supply or Jos won't survive!

**High Scores**

Jos Jaws features a Top 5 high-score table, where players can enter their name if they achieve a high enough score. Each player has their own personal best, with only their highest score being saved. Can you make it into the Top 5?

**How to Play**

**Level 1 – Find the Key**

**Aim**

- Find and collect the key to unlock Level 2.
- Avoid the jellyfish, which take away a life.
- Find the key before the underwater dangers become too much!

**How to Play**

- CH2: Moves Jos horizontally.
- CH3: Moves Jos vertically.
- Air Supply: Keep an eye on the air bar. If it runs out, it's Game Over.
- Scuba: Collect the scuba equipment to refill your air supply.

**Level 2 – Collect the Treasure**

**Aim**

- Collect as many treasure chests as possible.
- Avoid the sharks and jellyfish.
- Keep your air supply topped up.

**How to Play**

- CH2: Moves Jos horizontally.
- CH3: Moves Jos vertically.
- Air Supply: Keep an eye on the air bar. If it runs out, it's Game Over.
- Scuba: Collect the scuba equipment to refill your air supply.
- Treasure: Swim into a treasure chest to collect it.
- Sharks: Avoid the sharks. Colliding with one ends the game.
